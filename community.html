Our Commitment to the Community

At Crofton Dental Center, we are profoundly dedicated to the community we serve. Our passion for helping others doesn't stop at the edges of our patients' smiles; it extends into the neighborhoods, families, and organizations that make Crofton such an incredible place to call home.

We believe that true health encompasses the well-being of the entire community. From supporting local animal rescues to ensuring families have enough to eat, we are honored to use our platform and resources to give back, uplift, and support our neighbors in meaningful ways.

Our Key Initiatives

We partner with exceptional local organizations to maximize our impact and foster a culture of compassion.

Zoey's Legacy: SPCA Support

In loving memory of Zoey, Dr. Sood's beloved companion, Crofton Dental Center is a proud and active supporter of the Anne Arundel County SPCA. Zoey taught us about unconditional love and the immense joy that animals bring into our lives.

To honor her legacy, we regularly sponsor adoption events, donate much-needed supplies, and volunteer our time to help local shelter animals find their forever homes. We believe that caring for our community means advocating for those who do not have a voice.

Annual Food Drive

No family should ever have to worry about their next meal. Every holiday season, our team organizes a comprehensive community food drive to support local food banks and pantries throughout the Crofton area.

Thanks to the overwhelming generosity of our patients and staff, we successfully collect and distribute thousands of pounds of non-perishable goods, hygiene products, and essential items each year. This collective effort ensures that our neighbors in need receive crucial support during the most difficult times of the year.

Looking to the Future

Our commitment to Crofton is a continuously growing endeavor. As our practice expands, so does our capacity to give back. We are actively developing new scholarship programs for local students pursuing careers in healthcare, and we are establishing ongoing free-care days for underserved residents who urgently require dental treatments.

We invite our patients to join us in these efforts. When you choose Crofton Dental Center, you aren't just investing in your own health—you are actively contributing to a brighter, healthier future for the entire community.
