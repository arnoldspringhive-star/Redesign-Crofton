Every member of our staff has been hand-selected not only for their clinical expertise and professional achievements, but for their genuine compassion and warmth. We are a family of healthcare providers dedicated to making you feel like family, too.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Rahat Sood, DMD

Founder & Lead Dentist

Recognized as a leading dental expert, Dr. Sood is frequently featured on CNN, CNBC, and FOX News' Top Doctors Interview series. Her philosophy is deeply rooted in patient-centered care, bringing absolute clinical precision and genuine warmth to every single visit.

As a distinguished graduate of the Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Sood remains fiercely committed to continuing education. She stays at the very forefront of dental innovation, implementing 3D CBCT guided implant surgeries, laser dentistry, and digital workflows for minimally invasive care. Her passion is not just fixing teeth, but restoring her patients' confidence and quality of life.

Our Clinical Team

Our registered dental hygienists and certified dental assistants are the backbone of our clinical excellence. With decades of combined experience, they are passionate about patient education and preventive care.

During your visit, they will ensure you are comfortable, answer any questions you might have about your oral health, and provide gentle, thorough cleanings. They are your advocates in the treatment room, dedicated to making your experience entirely painless and surprisingly relaxing.

Our Patient Concierge

From the moment you call to schedule an appointment to the moment you check out, our dedicated patient concierge team is here to guide you. They are experts in navigating complex insurance plans, maximizing your benefits, and arranging flexible financing options.

We understand that the administrative side of healthcare can be stressful. Our concierge team completely removes that burden, handling all the paperwork on your behalf so that you can focus entirely on your health and your beautiful new smile.
