At Crofton Dental Center, we are relentless in our pursuit of clinical excellence. To provide you with the highest standard of care, we have fully integrated the latest advancements in dental technology into our practice.

By utilizing state-of-the-art equipment, we eliminate the guesswork from dentistry. Experience dentistry that is faster, safer, and infinitely more comfortable.

3D CBCT Cone Beam Imaging

Traditional X-rays only show a flat, 2D picture of your mouth. Our state-of-the-art 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scanner captures a highly detailed, 360-degree three-dimensional view of your teeth, jawbone, nerves, and soft tissues in a matter of seconds.

This technology is revolutionary for planning dental implants, performing root canals, and diagnosing complex issues that are entirely invisible to the naked eye. It ensures absolute precision and allows us to perform surgeries that are faster and significantly less invasive.

Digital Impression Systems

Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable, and gag-inducing putty impressions. We utilize advanced intraoral scanners to capture a perfect digital 3D model of your teeth in just a few minutes.

Our digital impression system is not only infinitely more comfortable for you, but it is also significantly more accurate than traditional molds. This means your crowns, veneers, and Invisalign clear aligners will fit perfectly on the very first try, saving you time and ensuring superior results.

Laser Dentistry

We have incorporated advanced dental lasers into our practice to perform a variety of soft tissue procedures with unprecedented comfort and precision. From gum reshaping to periodontal therapy, our lasers work quickly and quietly.

Laser dentistry minimizes bleeding, eliminates the need for sutures in many cases, and dramatically accelerates the healing process. It is the ultimate tool for providing gentle, minimally invasive care.

Personalized, compassionate dentistry in a warm and comforting environment. Combining advanced technology with a gentle touch to care for your smile.
