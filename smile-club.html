All plans include 15% off additional dental work, excluding orthodontic aligners and dental appliances. Membership clubs provided by Rahat Sood, DMD are payment arrangements, effective for twelve months from sign-up. Payment for treatment is due at the time of service. Membership dues must be current to receive services. Discounts cannot be combined with other offers or insurance. Patients must schedule and keep appointments; a $45 fee applies to cancellations with less than 48 hours’ notice.

Refunds are available within 30 days; no refunds after 30 days. Renewal fees are subject to change. Two or more missed appointments or late cancellations can result in plan termination without refund.
