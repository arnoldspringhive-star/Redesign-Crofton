Our Services

Sedation Dentistry, Relaxing, Stress-Free Comfort.

Dental anxiety is incredibly common, but it should never stand in the way of achieving optimal oral health. At Crofton Dental Center, we specialize in sedation dentistry to provide a completely stress-free, pain-free experience. Whether you require mild relaxation for a simple cleaning or deep sedation for a complex procedure, our expertly trained team ensures you remain safe, comfortable, and entirely at ease throughout your visit.

Relaxation Redefined

We understand that past negative experiences, sensitive teeth, or complex dental needs can make visiting the dentist feel overwhelming. That is why we have engineered our entire practice around your comfort. In addition to our spa-like atmosphere, noise-canceling headphones, and warm blankets, we offer a full spectrum of sedation options tailored specifically to your level of anxiety and the complexity of your treatment.

Our clinical team is extensively trained and certified in administering and monitoring dental sedation. Before any procedure, Dr. Sood will sit down with you to review your medical history in detail and help you select the sedation method that is safest and most effective for you.

Our Sedation Options

  • Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas): A mild, sweet-smelling gas that induces a deep sense of relaxation within minutes. The effects wear off immediately after the procedure, allowing you to drive yourself home.
  • Oral Conscious Sedation: Administered via a prescription pill taken prior to your appointment. You will remain awake and responsive, but deeply relaxed and unlikely to remember much of the procedure. (Requires a driver).
  • IV Sedation: Administered directly into the bloodstream for immediate, deep relaxation. Ideal for severe anxiety or lengthy, complex surgical procedures. (Requires a driver).

Who Can Benefit from Sedation?

  • Patients with high levels of dental anxiety or fear
  • Individuals with a severe gag reflex or highly sensitive teeth
  • Patients requiring multiple or complex procedures in a single visit
  • Those who have difficulty sitting still for extended periods
  • Anyone who wishes to have a more comfortable, memory-free dental experience

Safety First

  • Comprehensive pre-sedation medical review
  • State-of-the-art vital sign monitoring
  • Highly trained and certified clinical staff
  • Customized dosage for your exact needs
  • Continuous observation throughout recovery
