Our Services

Botox Therapeutics, Therapeutic and Aesthetic Relief.

Experience the therapeutic and aesthetic benefits of Botox at Crofton Dental Center. We offer specialized treatments to relieve TMJ pain and smooth facial lines.

Overview

While commonly known for its aesthetic benefits, Botox is a highly effective therapeutic tool in modern dentistry. As experts in facial anatomy, our team is uniquely qualified to administer Botox safely and precisely to treat both clinical and cosmetic concerns.

Clinically, we use Botox to relax the jaw muscles, providing profound relief for patients suffering from TMJ disorders, chronic teeth grinding (bruxism), and tension headaches. Aesthetically, it can be used to smooth fine lines and frame your radiant new smile perfectly.

The Process

  • Comprehensive evaluation of facial muscles and TMJ
  • Detailed discussion of your goals and symptoms
  • Precise, strategic application using a fine needle
  • Brief observation period
  • Follow-up appointment to assess optimal results

Key Benefits

  • Significant relief from TMJ pain and headaches
  • Reduces severe teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Non-surgical, minimally invasive treatment
  • Smoothes fine lines for a rejuvenated appearance
