Our Services

Dental Bridges, Restoring Your Complete Smile.

Bridge the gap in your smile seamlessly. Our custom dental bridges provide a sturdy, aesthetic solution to missing teeth, restoring both your bite and your confidence.

Overview

Missing teeth can lead to a host of dental problems, including the shifting of remaining teeth, bite misalignment, and an increased risk of gum disease. A dental bridge effectively literally bridges the gap created by one or more missing teeth, using the adjacent healthy teeth as anchors.

Our bridges are meticulously crafted from high-quality porcelain to ensure they look entirely natural and function flawlessly. We take great care in matching the shade and contour of your surrounding teeth for a seamless integration into your smile.

The Process

  • Preparation of the adjacent anchor teeth
  • Accurate digital impressions of your bite
  • Placement of a temporary bridge for protection
  • Custom fabrication by our expert ceramists
  • Permanent cementing and final polish

Key Benefits

  • Prevents surrounding teeth from shifting
  • Restores your ability to chew and speak naturally
  • Maintains the natural shape of your face
  • Aesthetic, custom-matched porcelain finish
