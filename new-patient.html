What to Expect on Your First Visit

Your very first visit to Crofton Dental Center is meticulously designed to be a comprehensive, deeply informative, and profoundly relaxing introduction to our luxury standard of care. We understand that visiting a new dental practice can often feel overwhelming, which is why we dedicate our time to ensuring your complete comfort from the moment you arrive. Here is what you can anticipate:

  1. A Warm, Guided Welcome: Enjoy a complimentary, artisanal beverage in our serene, spa-like lobby while our dedicated concierge team seamlessly assists you with any final necessary paperwork.
  2. Comprehensive Clinical Discovery: We conduct a remarkably thorough clinical examination, which includes advanced 3D CBCT imaging, precise digital intraoral scans, and a meticulous, life-saving oral cancer screening.
  3. Collaborative Consultation: Dr. Sood will sit down with you personally in a private setting to thoroughly review your diagnostic scans, attentively listen to your aesthetic and functional goals, and masterfully craft a bespoke, long-term treatment plan.
  4. Expert Hygiene Care: If scheduled during your initial visit, you will receive an exceptionally gentle, thorough professional cleaning by one of our highly skilled and compassionate hygienists.

Insurance & Financing

We fundamentally believe that world-class, luxury dental care should be financially accessible and entirely stress-free. We proudly accept most major PPO insurance plans and will complimentary file all necessary claims directly on your behalf to help maximize your entitled benefits. To accommodate your budget, we also offer highly flexible, low-to-no-interest financing options through trusted partners like CareCredit® and LendingClub.
