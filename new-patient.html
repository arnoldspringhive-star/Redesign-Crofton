Welcome to our practice.

We are thrilled to welcome you to the Crofton Dental Center family. Prepare to experience the luxury, compassion, and clinical excellence that define our practice.

What to Expect on Your First Visit

Your very first visit to Crofton Dental Center is meticulously designed to be a comprehensive, deeply informative, and profoundly relaxing introduction to our luxury standard of care.

  • A Warm, Guided Welcome: Enjoy a complimentary beverage in our serene lobby.
  • Comprehensive Clinical Discovery: We conduct a remarkably thorough clinical examination, including advanced 3D CBCT imaging.
  • Collaborative Consultation: Dr. Sood will sit down with you personally to craft a bespoke treatment plan.

Insurance & Financing

We fundamentally believe that world-class, luxury dental care should be financially accessible and entirely stress-free. We work with most major insurance carriers and file claims on your behalf to maximize your benefits.

To accommodate your budget, we also offer flexible, low-to-no-interest financing options through trusted partners. Review our sections below to submit an inquiry, request an appointment, or review new patient paperwork details.
