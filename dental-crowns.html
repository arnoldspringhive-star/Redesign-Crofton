Dental Crowns
Beautifully Delivered.

Restore the strength, function, and beauty of damaged teeth with our custom-crafted porcelain dental crowns, designed to blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

Book an Appointment

Overview

When a tooth is significantly decayed, cracked, or weakened, a dental crown provides essential protection and structural support. A crown completely encases the visible portion of the tooth, restoring its original shape, size, and strength while preventing further damage.

At Crofton Dental Center, we utilize only the finest dental porcelains to create crowns that are not only incredibly durable but also highly aesthetic, mimicking the light-reflecting properties of natural enamel so your restoration goes completely unnoticed.

The Process

  • Gentle preparation of the affected tooth
  • Precise digital impression of the area
  • Placement of a temporary protective crown
  • Custom fabrication at a premier dental laboratory
  • Final bonding and meticulous bite adjustment

Key Benefits

  • Protects and saves severely damaged teeth
  • Restores full chewing function
  • Custom color-matched to your natural teeth
  • Highly durable and long-lasting
