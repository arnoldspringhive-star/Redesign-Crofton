Our Services

Teeth Whitening, Brilliantly Radiant Results.

Erase years of stains and discoloration with our professional teeth whitening treatments. Achieve a brilliantly white smile safely, quickly, and comfortably.

Overview

Over time, our teeth naturally absorb stains from coffee, tea, wine, and certain foods, leading to a dull or yellowed appearance. Professional teeth whitening at Crofton Dental Center is the fastest, safest, and most effective way to rejuvenate your smile and restore its natural brilliance.

Unlike over-the-counter products that can cause sensitivity and deliver inconsistent results, our professional-grade whitening systems provide dramatic improvements while protecting your enamel and minimizing discomfort.

The Process

  • Initial shade assessment and goal setting
  • Application of protective gum barrier
  • Careful application of professional whitening gel
  • Activation with specialized light technology
  • Final shade comparison and aftercare instructions

Key Benefits

  • Dramatically brighter smile in one visit
  • Safe for enamel with minimal sensitivity
  • Customized to your desired shade
  • Boosts confidence instantly
